## Four questions to ask on every page

1. Is every price, promise, service area and policy true?
2. Is the next action obvious without reading the whole page?
3. On a phone, is any content hidden by the bottom action bar?
4. Does the copy sound like VVE Clean rather than generic marketing copy?

Automated checks cover structure and regressions; owner review is for truth,
tone and business policy.
