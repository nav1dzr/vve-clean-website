## Approved customer journey

The subsequent [customer refinements](customer-refinements.md) simplify Pricing, improve the EOT mobile hero, add a persistent quote basket and inline carpet steps, and display the newly verified Google score. Automatic Google refresh is implemented but awaits its provider configuration.

1. A customer chooses the service/items and requests a preferred time free. The saved reference is not a confirmed appointment.
2. Staff agree availability and edit service, scope/items, total, address, date, arrival window and preparation in CRM. Saving a draft does not replace a current offer.
3. Staff preview and send the agreed offer with a £30 deposit request. The normal business hold is 48 hours, with explicit earlier deadlines for near-term work. The deposit is part of the total.
4. A verified Stripe payment confirms the booking. The 48-hour hold is managed by the booking system: it is **not one 48-hour Stripe Checkout Session**. A shorter session can be renewed within the original hold, subject to the safe expiry cutoff.
5. The private booking page supports payment, rescheduling requests, cancellation and acceptance of revised paid bookings. The existing confirmed appointment remains in force while a proposed change awaits acceptance. Branded messages distinguish these states.
6. After cleaning, staff request the balance or record actual payment. Partial payments, refunds, delivery failures and payment conflicts remain visible. New website prices do not recalculate existing agreed bookings or issued invoices.

Availability is checked by staff, not an automatic capacity engine. Cancellation does not invent a fee or automatically issue a refund. Formal accounting documents remain in the existing invoice/receipt workflow.

The end-of-tenancy promise now provides a **seven-day reporting window** for missed agreed work and one covered return, arranged separately. It is neither a tenancy-deposit refund promise nor a deadline to perform the return. Complete and Tailored retain different scopes. No baseline service price, discount rule or £30 deposit amount changed.
