## Additional P1 finding: booking payments and invoice balances

A fully paid booking can still prefill a new invoice showing the full amount due. The booking and invoice payment ledgers are separate: `admin/src/pages/InvoiceEditorPage.tsx:72` copies only `depositAmount`, while `admin/api/_lib/invoiceLifecycle.js:171` initializes invoice paid amount to zero. The £100 phone-only booking had no deposit and a fully recorded £100 balance payment; the new-invoice form showed £100 due. Read-only evidence confirms the saved linked invoice remains a draft with £100 due. This is not another charge, and a draft is not an issued debt, but issuing it without reconciling the existing payment could mislead staff and the customer.

A narrow read-only warning now checks the linked booking's payment history on new-invoice creation and in the saved draft view, through `admin/src/components/BookingBalancePaymentNotice.tsx`. It explains that recorded balance payments are not automatically applied to the invoice and links back to the booking for review. If the check fails, it reports the uncertainty and offers retry; saving a draft remains possible. This warning does not reconcile money or impose an issuance guard. No automatic payment transfer, historical rewrite or financial rule change was made. Proposed follow-up: an explicit reconciliation decision before issuing, with any future payment transfer tied to a unique source-payment reference so it cannot be applied twice. Detailed evidence and exact references are in the local `booking-invoice-ledger-finding.md`.
