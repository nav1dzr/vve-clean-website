# VVE Clean brand and interface guide

## Positioning

VVE Clean should feel local, capable and easy to contact. The website is a booking tool, not a luxury brochure. It should help a visitor answer four questions quickly:

1. Do you provide the service I need?
2. Do you cover my postcode?
3. What will it cost or how is it quoted?
4. What happens after I submit the request?

## Voice

- Use plain British English and short sentences.
- State the scope, price and next action directly.
- Say "booking request" until availability is confirmed.
- Say "re-clean guarantee" only where the relevant package includes it.
- Avoid hype such as "best", "premium", "perfect", fake scarcity or guessed response times.
- Avoid medical, hygiene or stain-removal outcomes unless evidence supports the exact claim.
- Do not invent staff credentials, locations, reviews, dates or job results.

## Visual system

- Headings: Bricolage Grotesque, 700 to 800 weight.
- Body: Inter, 400 to 600 weight.
- Primary ink: `#10243E`.
- Action blue: `#0369A1`; hover `#075985`; dark `#0C4A6E`.
- Hero gradient: fresh teal `#0F766E` through action blue to `#0B7199`.
- Navy: `#020B24`.
- Muted text: `#5B6B7C`.
- Surface: `#F7FAFC`; line: `#DCE5EC`.
- WhatsApp action: `#075E54` with white text.
- Minimum tap target: 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- Form text on mobile: at least 16px.
- Motion: 150 to 300ms and disabled by `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Imagery

Use genuine VVE Clean work, team and equipment photos. Crop for the page subject and include a factual alt description. Do not use an unrelated job photo to imply a service or location. Keep originals outside `public`; publish compressed AVIF/WebP derivatives only.

## Conversion rules

- One dominant action per section.
- Keep Call and WhatsApp available without competing with the main quote action.
- Put price qualifiers next to the price, not in distant small print.
- Do not use fake booking notifications, countdowns or unverified "slots left" messages.
- Show FAQ text visibly whenever FAQ structured data is present.
